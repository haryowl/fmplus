# ARMADA M.1 — Promo pack (English)

*Last updated: 15 Oct 2026 — Modern theme, Cargo / Passenger / Sales desks, Goods & Customers master pages, profile-aware order CSV, New-stop rail popup, Live money & sales outcomes.*

Print the flyer as one A4 / letter page. Present the script in ~6–8 minutes. Pair with screenshots from Jobs, Goods, Dispatch Live, Field APK, and Manager Approve.

---

## A. One-page flyer

**ARMADA M.1**  
Dispatch & Maintenance — from plan to proof

Desk plans. Field executes. Manager closes the loop.

**One platform for three roles**

| Desk | Field (Android) | Manager |
|------|-----------------|--------|
| Plan routes, visits, rides, and workshop jobs | Do the work with GPS and camera | Approve and lock the record |

### Dispatch — *Plan the day. Assign the run. Deliver with proof.*

Inbox → job on a vehicle → WhatsApp to the driver → start / finish each stop → job complete

- **Three desks:** Cargo · Passenger · Sales — same Jobs board, profile-aware inbox
- Daily order pool: drop, pickup, or pickup-then-drop; **New stop opens as a popup** so the board stays readable
- Bulk **CSV import** for cargo, passenger (seats / luggage), and sales visits
- Dedicated **Goods & Stock** and **Customers** pages: catalog, pricing, depot stock, truck day-close, delivery points
- Print QR / barcode and desk Write NFC on goods; scan to add or confirm cargo on Field
- Optional serial / lot, vehicle tag, and depot location tag — Admin turns each on per Field role
- Auto-plan the fleet (CVRP) or assign by hand · capacity / seats aware
- Live board: pending, en route, delivered · money rollup · sales visit outcomes
- Proof: phone GPS, vehicle GPS, optional POD photo
- Themes: Warm · Mono · **Modern** (cool teal ops UI)
- Extra orders start a **new trip** — they never hang on a finished job

### Maintenance — *Schedule the service. Tech executes. Manager locks it.*

Create due → assign tech (WhatsApp) → Start → Done → Approve

- Schedule by date, kilometres, and/or engine hours
- Parts catalog with SKU, on-hand display; print QR / barcode and desk Write NFC
- Field scan to add parts (optional serial); optional require scan before Done
- Parts, labor, photos, and odometer on the job
- Next service cycle can open automatically after Done
- Reminders: due soon, overdue, next due — WhatsApp and email
- **Done is not closed** until the manager Approves

### Android Field app

One app for drivers and technicians.

- Dispatch and Maintenance tabs
- Month calendar with pending / in-progress / completed dots
- Navigate, start, finish, skip / reschedule
- Cargo, passenger, and sales visit flows on the same Field surface
- Scan: camera barcode / QR, type a code, or NFC tap (APK) — functions follow the Admin role matrix
- Camera or gallery for proof photos
- Share location while on duty — GPS continues with the screen off
- Sharing stops when the job is finished

**ARMADA M.1 — from plan to proof.**

## B. Six-slide speaker script (~6–8 minutes)

### Slide 1 — Title (45 seconds)

**On slide**  
ARMADA M.1  
Dispatch & Maintenance — plan, execute, close  
One platform · Desk + Field + Manager · Warm / Mono / Modern

**Say this**  
ARMADA M.1 is not another GPS map. You already know where the vehicle is. This is the work layer: what must be delivered, who to visit, who to pick up, what must be serviced, who does it, and the proof when it is done. Three surfaces: the desk plans, the Android Field app executes, the manager closes the loop. The desk UI can stay Warm, go Mono, or switch to Modern — a cleaner teal ops look — without changing how the work runs.

---

### Slide 2 — Who uses it (60 seconds)

**On slide**  
Desk plans. Field executes. Manager closes the loop.

| Role | Place | Job |
|------|--------|-----|
| Dispatcher | Jobs + Dispatch Live | Plan the day |
| Catalog / points | Goods · Customers | Stock and delivery points |
| Maintenance desk | Maintenance | Schedule service |
| Driver / technician | Field app `/m` | Do the work |
| Manager | Manager `/mm` | Approve and lock |

**Say this**  
Each customer gets only the modules they buy — Dispatch, Maintenance, or both. Inside Dispatch you can enable Cargo, Passenger, Sales, or a mix; the same Jobs board switches desk mode. Field staff see both Dispatch and Maintenance tabs on one phone when both are enabled. Managers do not share the driver login; they have their own Approve screen. That is how you keep planning, execution, and sign-off separate.

---

### Slide 3 — Dispatch (90 seconds)

**On slide**  
Plan the day. Assign the run. Deliver with proof.

Inbox orders → Job on vehicle → WhatsApp → START / FINISH → Complete

- Cargo · Passenger · Sales desks · New-stop popup · profile CSV
- Goods & Customers pages · depot stock · day-close · rates
- Auto-plan or manual assign · capacity / seats
- Scan cargo · print QR · desk NFC · Live money & sales outcomes
- GPS + optional POD · Modern theme optional

**Say this**  
The dispatcher builds today’s inbox — pin the map, open New stop in a popup so the board does not stretch, import a CSV for cargo, passenger seats, or sales visits, or generate daily routines. Orders go onto a vehicle job with cube, kilo, or seat capacity. Goods and customers live on their own centered desk pages: catalog, pricing, depot stock, truck day-close. Goods get a SKU: print a QR or barcode, or write an NFC tag from Chrome on Android. On Field the driver scans to add cargo, confirm a checklist, or record a serial — Admin chooses which actions each role gets. Assign the driver and they get WhatsApp with a link into Field — including fare or goods copy when you use those flows. They navigate, start, and finish each stop. Phone and vehicle GPS are stored; you can require a photo. Sales visits collect an outcome on Finish. When the last stop is done, the job closes. If more orders arrive, you start a new trip — you do not reopen a finished run. Ops watches Dispatch Live: status, money rollup, and sales outcomes.

**Demo if you have time:** Modern theme → New-stop popup with a catalog good → or import a short passenger/sales CSV → assign → finish on Field.

---

### Slide 4 — Maintenance (90 seconds)

**On slide**  
Schedule the service. Tech executes. Manager locks it.

Create due → Assign (WhatsApp) → Start → Done → Approve

- Date / km / engine hours
- Parts catalog: SKU, print QR / NFC, on-hand display
- Field scan parts · optional serial · optional require scan before Done
- Next cycle after Done · reminders: due soon / overdue / next due

**Say this**  
Maintenance is a service event on a vehicle, not a delivery stop. You schedule by calendar, odometer, hours, or all three. Parts share the same scan foundation as goods: print at the desk, scan on the phone. Admin can require every catalog part to be scanned before Done — the beep never auto-completes the job. Assign the technician — WhatsApp again. They start the job, log notes, parts, labor, odometer, and photos. When they tap Done, that is not the end. The manager reviews and Approves. That lock is the audit trail. If the job has a cycle, the next due can be created automatically, and we remind the desk before it is late.

**Contrast line:** Dispatch is finished when the driver finishes the route. Maintenance is finished when the manager Approves.

### Slide 5 — Same Field app, two kinds of work (75 seconds)

**On slide**

| | Dispatch | Maintenance |
|--|----------|-------------|
| Unit | Job + stops (cargo / passenger / sales) | Service event |
| Finished means | Driver completed the route / visit | Tech done; manager Approves |
| Scan | Cargo add / confirm / serial · vehicle · depot | Part add / serial · vehicle · require before Done |
| Labels | Print QR · desk NFC write | Same on parts catalog |
| Repeat | Routine templates | Next due after Done |
| Incomplete | Skip / reschedule same order | Skip / reopen |
| Notify | Assign · fare / goods WhatsApp | Assign + due reminders |
| Phone | Start / finish + POD + scan | Start / Done + parts photos + scan |

**Say this**  
This is why customers buy both. The same Android app, two tabs, one scan switchboard per role — driver, operator, worker, field staff. A driver delivering, a passenger run, a sales visit, and a technician in the workshop do not learn two products. Camera, typed code, or NFC tap on the APK all hit the same catalog. The difference is governance: a delivery or visit can close in the field; a service job waits for the manager. That is the sentence to leave in the room.

## Design notes

- Flyer: wordmark + two small screenshots (Jobs board with Modern theme, Field finish). Do not put the comparison table on the printed page.
- Slides: keep on-slide text large; the script carries the detail. Prefer Modern-theme screenshots for the current product look.
- Do not claim Play Store, iOS, or tracking when no job is in progress.
- Do not claim Field writes NFC, scan auto-completes a job, or scan decrements inventory — those stay desk write / gate / display-only (depot stock adjust is a separate desk action).
- Scan depth for engineers: `docs/ARMADA-M1-Scan-Phases.md`.
